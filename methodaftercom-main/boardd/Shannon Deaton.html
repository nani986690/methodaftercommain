Board Chair

Shannon Deaton

A passionate advocate for children and families, Shannon has been an integral part of the Method community for nearly two decades, providing leadership, service, and unwavering support to help every child thrive.

About Shannon Deaton

Shannon's relationship with Method Child Development Center began in 2006 when her son enrolled as an infant. Since then, Method has remained an important part of her family's journey, with both of her children benefiting from the nurturing environment that helped shape their growth and development.

Since joining the Board of Directors in 2010, Shannon has served in several leadership positions, including Parent Group Leader, Board Chair, Vice Chair, and Community Service Chair. She played an important role in establishing the Jan Sharp Scholarship Fund, helping provide affordable childcare opportunities for families facing financial challenges.

Beyond her work with Method, Shannon is passionate about connecting community organizations, youth volunteers, and local partners to strengthen Method's mission. She values meaningful relationships with staff and believes these connections create a positive, welcoming environment for children and families alike.

Shannon earned her Bachelor's degree in Biological Engineering, along with minors in Environmental Science and Communications, from NC State University. She later completed a Master's degree in Natural Resources and is a North Carolina Registered Professional Engineer.

With more than 26 years of public service, she currently serves as Chief of the Habitat Conservation Division for the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission, leading a statewide team dedicated to protecting natural resources and supporting conservation initiatives.

Shannon firmly believes in Method's mission of embracing families from every background and creating an inclusive environment where every child has the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive together.
