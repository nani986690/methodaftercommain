WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE METHOD

What Parents Tell Us Matters Most

Based on our 2026 parent survey.

Convenient & Community-Rooted Location

“Location, non-corporate — the teachers are like family.” — 2026 Parent Survey

Centrally located at 900 Trailwood Drive in Raleigh, Method offers an accessible neighborhood campus close to NC State and primary commute corridors. In our annual parent surveys, families consistently emphasize that our convenient physical location and warm, community-first environment are key reasons they choose Method.

Nonprofit Care & Community First

Method is a nonprofit childcare center founded in 1969 and guided by an all-volunteer Board of Directors. We operate year-round to support working families without public school schedule gaps.

“I am so appreciative that we do not follow the WCPSS calendar. This shows that our Center truly serves families.”

Certified Natural Learning Playground

“The playground is great. I especially appreciate the natural materials over turf/rubberized surfaces.”

Partnered with the Natural Learning Initiative, our outdoor environment features real trees, grass, sensory gardens, pathways, and natural play materials that encourage exploration and physical confidence.

Dedicated & Loving Staff

“We feel so lucky to have found Method. The teachers are loving and patient.”

“He is always excited to come to school to see his teacher and classmates.”

With experienced educators and low, age-tailored staff-to-child ratios across our 6 classrooms, children receive attentive, individualized care and daily kindergarten readiness in a warm, family-like environment:
