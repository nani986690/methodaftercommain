JAN SHARP MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP

Jan Sharp Memorial Scholarship Fund

The Jan Sharp Memorial Scholarship Fund honors Jan Sharp, a dedicated Board member who passed away while serving Method. We are proud to continue her vision for the Center by providing need-based scholarships so every child has access to quality early childhood education. Visit the official Jan Sharp Fund Facebook page for community updates and announcements.
