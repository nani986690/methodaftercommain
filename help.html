JAN SHARP MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP

Jan Sharp Memorial Scholarship Fund

The Jan Sharp Memorial Scholarship Fund honors Jan Sharp, a dedicated Board member who passed away while serving Method. We are proud to continue her vision for the Center by providing need-based scholarships so every child has access to quality early childhood education.

  • Support children in need
  • Expand educational opportunities
  • Strengthen our community
  • Invest in the future of every child
